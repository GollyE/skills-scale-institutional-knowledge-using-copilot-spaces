# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define the approach for validating product quality and coordinate testing throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Establish test strategy, coverage, and acceptance validation plans
- Coordinate test execution and communicate defects and quality risks
- Work with Developers to improve testability and resolve defects
- Confirm release quality evidence and readiness criteria

### Goals
- Identify issues early and reduce production defects
- Provide clear, repeatable evidence that requirements are met
- Make quality risks visible before release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Coordinate with Developers on test coverage, defects, and retesting
- Share quality status and release-readiness risks with Project Managers

---

## Technical / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Technical / Engineering Leads guide technical decisions and help the team deliver maintainable solutions that meet product needs.

### Responsibilities
- Set or clarify technical direction, architecture, and engineering standards
- Assess technical feasibility, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Review significant design and implementation decisions
- Support Developers with technical guidance and unblock complex work

### Goals
- Deliver secure, reliable, maintainable technical solutions
- Balance product needs with technical quality and long-term sustainability
- Surface technical risks and trade-offs early

### Typical Communication
- Discuss feasibility, options, and trade-offs with Product Managers
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and reviews
- Report technical dependencies, risks, and progress to Project Managers

---

## Release / Deployment Managers

### Role Summary
Release / Deployment Managers coordinate the safe, predictable promotion of changes into production.

### Responsibilities
- Plan deployment timing, sequencing, and release communications
- Coordinate readiness checks, approvals, and dependencies
- Confirm rollback plans and deployment ownership
- Track deployment outcomes and escalate release blockers

### Goals
- Deliver changes safely with minimal disruption
- Make release responsibilities and decisions clear
- Ensure teams are prepared to respond if a deployment fails

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate deployment steps and technical support with Developers
- Confirm scope and customer-facing timing with Product Managers
- Track readiness, dependencies, and decisions with Project Managers

---

## Stakeholders / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Business Owners represent business needs and provide direction or decisions on outcomes, priorities, and scope.

### Responsibilities
- Explain business goals, constraints, and expected outcomes
- Provide timely decisions on priorities and scope trade-offs
- Validate whether delivered outcomes meet business needs
- Identify affected groups and communicate relevant business context

### Goals
- Achieve measurable business outcomes and customer value
- Ensure delivery decisions reflect business priorities
- Maintain visibility into progress, risks, and results

### Typical Communication
- Work with Product Managers on priorities, success measures, and outcome validation
- Share context and timely decisions with Project Managers for planning and escalation
- Consult with Developers on technical implications when evaluating trade-offs

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Representatives advise on production readiness and help teams support and operate delivered services.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational requirements, monitoring needs, and support impacts
- Review runbooks, alerting, and incident-response readiness
- Share customer issues and operational feedback with delivery teams
- Assist with incident triage and post-release follow-up

### Goals
- Keep services stable, observable, and supportable
- Reduce time to detect and resolve production issues
- Ensure support teams are prepared for changes

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate operational requirements and incident fixes with Developers
- Share customer-impact and service feedback with Product Managers
- Raise readiness concerns, incidents, and follow-up actions with Project Managers
