# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation set describes how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, and improves cross-functional projects. Use it as a shared reference for consistent, customer-focused delivery and clear team ownership.

## Project Management Process

- **Initiation:** Validate the problem, goals, success measures, stakeholders, timeline, and initial risks in a project one-pager. Align on a go/no-go decision before planning.
- **Planning:** Turn approved work into shippable increments with a prioritized, estimated backlog, acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, dependencies, and a QA approach.
- **Execution and tracking:** Coordinate work on a project board, surface progress and blockers in team standups and delivery syncs, and review work through small pull requests, automated checks, and QA.
- **Risk management and communication:** Maintain and review a shared risk register; send regular stakeholder updates, keep project status in a single source of truth, and escalate blockers from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed.
- **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback plan; deploy and verify the release, then communicate it to stakeholders.
- **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** Hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents. Assign owners and due dates to a small set of improvement actions and follow up on them.

The **Project Manager** coordinates delivery, schedule, risks, and communication; the **Product Manager** owns outcomes and backlog priorities; **Developers** build and test; **QA/Testing** validates quality and acceptance criteria; and **Stakeholders** provide input and approvals.
